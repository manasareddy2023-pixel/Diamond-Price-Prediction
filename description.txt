# Diamond Price Prediction Dataset

**Dataset Name:** Diamonds Dataset

**Description:**

This dataset contains information about diamonds and their prices.

**Target Variable:**
- price: Price of the diamond in US dollars.

**Input Features:**
1. carat - Weight of the diamond.
2. cut - Quality of the diamond cut.
3. color - Diamond color grade.
4. clarity - Diamond clarity grade.
5. depth - Total depth percentage.
6. table - Width of the top of the diamond.
7. x - Length in mm.
8. y - Width in mm.
9. z - Depth in mm.

**Machine Learning Algorithm:**
K-Nearest Neighbors Regressor (KNN).

**Evaluation Metrics:**
- Mean Absolute Error (MAE)
- Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE)
- R² Score

**Application:**
Diamond Price Prediction using Streamlit.
